## 1. S4 selection: degenerate in our motif space, as predicted

| Diagnostic | Value |
|---|---|
| Candidate pool | 25 unlinked motif pairs (S1's pool), literature ≤ 2016 |
| Works per motif concept up to 2016 | layering 73, mixing_service 45, fan_out 11, smurfing 9, pass_through 6, coinjoin 2, fan_in 1, peel_chain 0 |
| Pairs with **zero** bridging authors | **24 of 25** (only 2 distinct availability values) |
| S4 overlap with S1 / with S2 | 4 / 4 of 10 |
| Vendi diversity (effective distinct pairs) | S1 8.01, S2 7.83, S4 7.97 |

**What this means.** With 24 of 25 pairs tied at "no bridging author", the unavailability term is almost constant.
S4 is therefore essentially a **coherence ranking**, not an alien selection. The paper's mechanism needs a community
record that our 8 motif concepts do not have. This confirms both the concern in `docs/alien-space-evaluation.md` and
Codex's G06. S4 stays pre-registered for the Stage 2 run, but it should be read as "coherence-ranked motif pairs".

## 2. Temporal validation on the AML literature (2013 → 2016)

Setup:
- the vocabulary was chosen with works up to 2013 only (G06);
- **82,576** unlinked node pairs in 2013, of which **5,442** became linked in 2014–2016;
- 1,893 of those links had no bridging author in 2013 ("far");
- shortlist K = 1% (826 pairs); enrichment = hits ÷ chance.

| β | Availability: all future links | Availability: far links | Far share of hits | Rarity control: all | Rarity control: far |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (coherence only) | 3.40× | 2.90× | 30% | 3.40× | 2.90× |
| 0.2 | 3.05× | 3.38× | 39% | 2.99× | 2.69× |
| 0.4 | 2.77× | 4.70× | 59% | 1.91× | 1.69× |
| 0.6 | 2.57× | 6.07× | 82% | 0.33× | 0.63× |
| 0.7 | 2.30× | 6.28× | 95% | 0.22× | 0.48× |
| 1.0 (unavailability only) | 0.20× | 0.58× | 100% | 0.00× | 0.00× |

**Other measures:**
- **The scores are distinct.** Spearman(coherence, availability) = 0.10, and Spearman(availability, minimum concept
  frequency) = 0.23.
- **Within the zero-bridging stratum,** coherence alone still finds future links at **4.34×** chance.
- **Within coverage quintiles** (matched on minimum concept frequency), adding availability *lowers* recovery of all
  future links. At β = 0.7 the quintile enrichments are 2.1–4.8×, against 3.2–4.8× for coherence alone.

**Reading:**
1. **Coherence alone predicts future links** in this literature at 3.4× chance. This is consistent with the paper's
   β = 0 result (35.9× on its own corpus; not comparable in size).
2. **Unavailability is not rarity.** Swapping unavailability for rarity destroys recovery: 0.22× against 2.30× at
   β = 0.7.
3. **Weighting unavailability moves the shortlist toward links no community bridged** (the far share rises from 30%
   to 95%), at a moderate cost to overall recovery. This is the paper's qualitative pattern. But the "far" outcome is
   defined by the same signal (zero bridging authors), so its rising enrichment is **partly by construction**. The
   circularity-free evidence is narrower: inside the far stratum, future links are still predictable from coherence
   (4.34×).
4. **Whether far links are worth more** (more novel, more useful, harder for models) is **not tested here**. That is
   the question for Codex's E03 design (arm D against arm C).

**Limits:**
- These are point estimates with no intervals.
- There is one corpus (AML literature) and one split.
- 9% of works have no author records.
- The lightweight scorers are adaptations, not the paper's trained models.
